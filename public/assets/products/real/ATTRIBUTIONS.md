# Product image sources

The accessory product cutouts in this folder come from official Apple product imagery:

- `charger-usb-c.png`: Apple 20W USB-C Power Adapter (`MWVV3`)
- `cable-usb-c.png`: Apple 60W USB-C Charge Cable (`MW493`)
- `cable-lightning.png`: Apple USB-C to Lightning Cable (`MUQ93`)
- `coque-iphone.png`: Apple iPhone case (`MYY13`)
- `verre-trempe.png`: Belkin screen protector sold by Apple (`HPQE2`)
- `ecouteurs.png`: Apple AirPods Pro (`MTJV3`)
- `batterie-externe.png`: Apple MagSafe Battery Pack (`MJWY3`)
- `support-voiture.png`: Belkin car mount sold by Apple (`HRX82`)

Device images:

- `iphone-15.png`: Wikimedia Commons, file `IPhone 15.png`
